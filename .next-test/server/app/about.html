Our story

Where it began: a peoples hospital since 1971

In 1971, the people of Kochi came together to build a hospital they could all rely on, whatever their means. Founded by the Cochin Co-operative Hospitals Society, Indira Gandhi Co-operative Hospital (IGCH), Kadavanthra, Kochi, has grown into an affordable multispeciality co-operative hospital in Kochi, with 30 medical departments. More than fifty years later, it continues to follow the same belief: every patient deserves equal respect and quality healthcare, with additional support for those who need it most.

Every patient receives the same respect, dignity and commitment to quality care, regardless of their financial circumstances.

We provide additional support to those who need it most through free and subsidised cancer screening, affordable treatment and free dialysis services.

IGCH: Growth, Achievements and Recognition

Since its establishment in 1971, Indira Gandhi Co-operative Hospital (IGCH) has expanded its medical services, developed its facilities and introduced new healthcare initiatives. Its progress includes advancements in specialised medical care, community health programmes, patient support services and institutional development.

Medical Care and Achievements

  • 15,000+ Haemodialysis sessions delivered to patients.
  • 7,000+ Free dialysis sessions provided to economically disadvantaged patients.
  • 200 Cardiothoracic surgeries performed within 15 months.
  • 5G Introduction of Kerala's first 5G Smart Ambulance.
