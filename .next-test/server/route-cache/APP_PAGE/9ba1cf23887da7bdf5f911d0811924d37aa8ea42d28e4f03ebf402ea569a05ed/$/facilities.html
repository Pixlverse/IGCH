Facilities & Services

Along with medical and surgical specialities, the hospital offers a range of facilities to support patients through emergency care, diagnosis, treatment and recovery.

5G AmbulanceEmergency · ICU · OTDialysisDiagnosticsPharmacyInsurance & TPACanteen

Established in 1971, Indira Gandhi Co-operative Hospital (IGCH) was founded on the belief that quality healthcare should be accessible to everyone, irrespective of wealth.

Located at Kadavanthra, Kochi, IGCH has grown into a multispeciality hospital serving patients from Kochi, Ernakulam and neighbouring districts of Kerala.

A first for Kerala

5G Smart Ambulance

Emergency care, even before reaching the hospital.

IGCH's 5G Smart Ambulance connects patients with the hospital's emergency team while they are still on the road. Patient vitals and medical information can be shared during transit, helping the team prepare for the patient's arrival.

Key features
  • Real-time patient vital monitoring
  • Live video communication
  • Remote medical assessment
  • Other verified onboard emergency equipment

Dialysis Unit

Making dialysis care more accessible.

We provide haemodialysis services for patients with kidney failure. The hospital has also provided more than 7,000 free dialysis sessions to economically disadvantaged patients.

Rehabilitation & Supportive Care

Care that continues beyond medical treatment.

Recovery and long-term health management may require support beyond consultations and medication. IGCH offers clinical support services to address patients' physical, nutritional and psychological needs.

Physiotherapy

Physiotherapy services support rehabilitation, mobility and recovery following illness, injury or surgery.

Dietetics

Dietary guidance to help patients manage nutritional requirements in line with their medical conditions and treatment plans.

Clinical Psychology

Psychological assessment and support for patients who need help managing emotional and psychological concerns.

Pain & Palliative Care

Support for patients living with serious or long-term illnesses, with attention to pain management, symptom relief and quality of life.

Insurance & TPA

Insurance & TPA Support

Help with insurance approvals and hospital payments. IGCH works with insurance companies and Third-Party Administrators (TPAs) to facilitate cashless treatment for eligible patients. The hospital's insurance desk assists patients and families with pre-authorisation, documentation and insurance-related enquiries.

Insurance & Payment Information
  1. 1

    Check eligibility

    Confirm your policy or scheme covers the treatment.

  2. 2

    Share documents

    Health insurance card or CGHS card, as applicable, valid photo ID, previous medical reports and investigation results, and a referral letter (for CGHS patients).

  3. 3

    Pre-authorisation

    Our desk coordinates approval with your insurer or TPA.

  4. 4

    Cashless care

    Treatment proceeds, with claims settled directly.

Cashless treatment is subject to policy coverage, terms and approval from the insurer or TPA.
