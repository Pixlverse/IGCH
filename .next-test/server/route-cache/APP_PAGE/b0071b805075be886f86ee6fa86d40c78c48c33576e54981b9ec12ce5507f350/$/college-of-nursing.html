Indira Gandhi College of Nursing, Kochi

A four-year B.Sc. Nursing course, taught inside a multispeciality hospital.

Explore Admissions

Indira Gandhi College of Nursing, established in 2006 at Kadavanthra, Kochi, offers a four-year Bachelor of Science in Nursing programme. Students receive academic instruction at the college and clinical training at Indira Gandhi Co-operative Hospital (IGCH), gaining experience in different areas of patient care.

The college is affiliated with Kerala University of Health Sciences and approved by the Indian Nursing Council and the Kerala Nursing Council.

Why choose Indira Gandhi College of Nursing?

Clinical training at a multi-speciality hospital

Students gain clinical experience at Indira Gandhi Co-operative Hospital, with exposure to different medical and surgical departments. Their training includes patient care in general wards, OPD, ICU, CCU, operation theatres and emergency services.

Learning across specialised departments

The hospital's specialist departments provide students with exposure to different areas of nursing practice, including heart care, cancer care and dialysis and kidney care.

A co-operative healthcare environment

IGCH is managed by Cochin Co-operative Hospitals Society Ltd. The hospital follows a co-operative approach to healthcare, with a focus on accessible treatment and patient support. Students learn in an environment where patient care and community health are important parts of hospital practice.

Faculty, hostel and campus facilities

Students are guided by experienced nursing faculty and have access to classrooms, a library, computer facilities, hostel accommodation and a dining facility. The hostel is supervised by a warden, and transport is provided for community health postings.

Clinical Training at IGCH

Clinical training at a multispeciality hospital gives nursing students opportunities to learn about different conditions, treatments and patient care requirements.

Some of the key areas of exposure include:

Heart Care

Students gain exposure to cardiology and cardiothoracic and vascular surgery, including patient care before and after cardiac procedures.

Explore Heart Care

Cancer Care

Training includes exposure to medical and surgical oncology, chemotherapy-related care and the nursing support required by cancer patients and their families.

Explore Cancer Care

Dialysis and Kidney Care

Students learn about related conditions and the nursing care involved in dialysis and the management of patients with kidney disease.

Explore Dialysis and Kidney Care

Other clinical postings include emergency care, ICU, operation theatre, obstetrics, paediatrics and general medicine.

Campus Facilities

The college provides facilities to support academic learning and student life, including:

  • Classrooms and smart learning facilities
  • Library with nursing textbooks and journals
  • Computer laboratory
  • Experienced nursing faculty
  • Warden-supervised hostel
  • Dining facility and canteen
  • Transport for community health postings
  • Anti-Ragging Cell

Admission to B.Sc. Nursing

Admission to the B.Sc. Nursing programme is through LBS allotment, subject to the applicable eligibility criteria and admission guidelines.

Students can refer to the latest admission notifications and college prospectus for information about eligibility, application procedures, fees and available seats.

For admission enquiries, contact the college office.
