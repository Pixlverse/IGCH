Broad Specialities at Indira Gandhi Co-operative Hospital (IGCH), Kadavanthra, Kochi

As a co-operative hospital in Kochi, bringing specialist healthcare closer to the people of Kochi.

Super SpecialitiesBroad SpecialitiesClinical Support

Indira Gandhi Co-operative Hospital (IGCH), Kadavanthra, Kochi, brings together 14 broad speciality departments to address a wide range of healthcare needs. From general medicine and surgery to women's health, children's care and mental health, patients can access different medical specialities within one hospital.
