Clinical Support Services at Indira Gandhi Co-operative Hospital, Kochi

The medical teams and services that support diagnosis, treatment and recovery.

Super SpecialitiesBroad SpecialitiesClinical Support
7clinical support departments

At Indira Gandhi Co-operative Hospital (IGCH), Kadavanthra, Kochi, clinical support departments work alongside our medical and surgical specialities at different stages of patient care. From emergency medicine and diagnostic imaging to anaesthesia, physiotherapy and nutritional support, these services help doctors assess conditions, plan treatment and support recovery.

Explore our seven clinical support departments to learn about the services available at IGCH.

Emergency Medicine Available 24/7

Medical help in Kochi when every minute matters.

Our emergency team is available around the clock in Kochi to assess and manage medical emergencies, supported by ICU facilities, operation theatres and a blood bank on the hospital campus.

Support across every stage of treatment

Clinical support services work alongside the hospital's medical and surgical departments.

  1. Before treatment

    Diagnostic imaging and laboratory investigations help doctors assess a patient's condition.

  2. During treatment

    Anaesthesiology supports surgical procedures and patient monitoring.

  3. During emergencies

    Emergency Medicine provides immediate medical assessment and care.

  4. During recovery

    Physiotherapy and dietetics help patients work towards better mobility, strength and nutritional health.

At IGCH, these departments support the wider range of healthcare services available at our hospital in Kadavanthra, Kochi.
