Quick answers

Questions patients ask us most.

Is IGCH empanelled under CGHS?

Yes. IGCH is CGHS-empanelled and NABH-accredited (Entry Level), and accepts referrals from CGHS wellness centres. In an emergency, come directly — no referral needed.

Is the emergency department open 24 hours?

Yes — emergency, ICU, operation theatre and blood bank run 24/7. Call 0484 294 1600.

Is parking available at the hospital?

Parking on campus is limited, and valet service is available. [Parking tips and nearby options — to confirm]
